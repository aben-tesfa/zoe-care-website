Meet Zoe Care

Support that starts with understanding.

We believe that the best care helps people feel seen, respected, and empowered in their own lives.

Who we are

A caring partner in everyday life.

Zoe Care, LLC is a Colorado-based home care and support services agency dedicated to helping individuals live safely, comfortably, and independently in their homes and communities.

We focus on non-medical personal care, homemaker services, in-home support services, and support for individuals with intellectual and developmental disabilities through applicable authorized programs.

At every step, we aim to put the individual’s voice, preferences, comfort, and goals first.

Our approach

Guided by compassion. Grounded in respect.

The values we bring to every interaction.

Compassion

We approach every person with kindness, patience, and care.

Dignity

We respect privacy, personal choices, and individual needs.

Independence

We encourage confidence, autonomy, and meaningful daily living.

Community

We value connection, inclusion, and a sense of belonging.

Our promise

Personalized support, not a one-size-fits-all solution.

We recognize that care is personal. Our goal is to understand what matters to each individual and work together to identify suitable services, subject to eligibility and service availability.
