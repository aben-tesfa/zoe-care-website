What we offer

The right help can make all the difference.

Explore the kinds of practical, respectful support Zoe Care can provide based on individual needs, authorization, and service availability.

01 / IN-HOME SUPPORT

In-Home Support Services (IHSS)

For eligible individuals, IHSS can help make remaining in their homes and communities safer and more comfortable through authorized support.

  • Assistance with approved daily living needs
  • Support intended to maintain comfort and independence
  • Services guided by applicable program requirements

Eligibility and covered services depend on the relevant program and approval.

02 / DAILY PERSONAL CARE

Personal Care Services

Respectful assistance with essential activities, provided in a way that protects dignity, comfort and individual choice.

  • Bathing, grooming, and personal hygiene support
  • Dressing and toileting assistance
  • Mobility and transfer assistance, as authorized
  • Help with everyday routines
  • Supporting safety, comfort, and independence
03 / A MORE COMFORTABLE HOME

Homemaker Services

A clean, organized home can make daily life less stressful. Homemaker support focuses on everyday household tasks.

  • Light housekeeping
  • Laundry and linen changes
  • Meal preparation
  • Kitchen cleaning and household organization
  • Grocery shopping and errands, when authorized
  • Maintaining a clean and safe home environment
04 / DEVELOPMENTAL DISABILITY SUPPORT

Developmental Disability (DD) Waiver Services

Through applicable Program Approved Service Agency (PASA) arrangements, authorized supports may be available for individuals with intellectual and developmental disabilities.

  • Person-centered support
  • Community inclusion
  • Development of daily living skills
  • Support for greater independence

Service delivery is subject to agency approval, individual eligibility, waiver rules, and authorized service scope. Specific offerings should be confirmed with Zoe Care.
