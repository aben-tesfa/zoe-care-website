Ask Us a Question
What types of services does Zoe Care offer?

Our service categories include In-Home Support Services (IHSS), personal care services, homemaker services, and authorized supports under applicable developmental disability programs. Availability depends on approvals, location, eligibility, and service scope.

Do you provide medical or nursing care?

The information on this website focuses on non-medical home care and support services. Please contact Zoe Care for clarification about the exact authorized scope of any service.

Do you serve all Colorado counties?

Zoe Care is Colorado-based. Current service availability varies by county, staffing, program eligibility, and applicable authorizations. Contact us with your county to confirm.

How do I request care?

Use the Request Care form to share your name, contact information, county, and service interest. Once the secure form service is activated, the team can follow up about next steps.

Can a family member or support coordinator reach out?

Yes. Family members, guardians, and support coordinators may contact Zoe Care to ask about care options. Personal details and decisions should be handled with appropriate permissions and respect for privacy.

Is everyone eligible for IHSS or waiver services?

No. Eligibility and covered services depend on the applicable Colorado program, assessment, authorization, and other requirements. An inquiry does not guarantee approval or services.

How can I apply for a caregiver position?

Visit the Careers page to share an initial inquiry. Specific vacancies, required qualifications, background checks, training, and work locations must be confirmed by the hiring team.

Can I book an appointment online?

You can request an initial conversation using the contact form. Live scheduling and appointment booking may be added once a booking service is configured.
