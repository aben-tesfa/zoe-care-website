Privacy & transparency

Your privacy matters.

Information about how this demonstration website is set up, and what must be finalized before launch.

Draft notice: This page must be reviewed and completed by Zoe Care before the site becomes public. It is not a finished legal privacy policy.

Information collected through forms

The website includes inquiry forms requesting names, email addresses, phone numbers, counties, service interests, and optional messages. In this website preview, the forms do not transmit or save submissions.

Information you should not submit

Please avoid entering medical history, diagnoses, government identification numbers, insurance details, or other sensitive health information through these initial inquiry forms.

Production website requirements

Before launching, Zoe Care should confirm its actual data-collection practices, retention period, recipient systems, access controls, privacy contact information, cookie/analytics practices, and legal obligations. Any handling of protected health information must use appropriately configured systems and agreements where required.
